Academic Philosophy

At Morang Model we combine strong classroom teaching with practical learning, values education and co-curricular activities. Our aim is to prepare students not only for board examinations but for higher education and responsible citizenship.

We follow the curriculum of the National Examination Board (NEB) for school and +2 levels, and Tribhuvan University for the BBS program.
